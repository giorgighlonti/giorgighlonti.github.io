ABOUT ME

High-impact design for brands that refuse to blend in, from emails to 3d, and everything in between.

I'm a graphic designer with 5+ years of experience and co-founder of mailio, an email marketing agency helping e-commerce brands turn their inbox into a real revenue channel.

I create email designs, automated flows, brand identities, 3d product graphics, and web layouts that help brands communicate faster and convert better.

My focus is clean design, strong visual hierarchy, and creative direction that feels premium without being overcomplicated — built in Figma, photoshop, illustrator, xd, and blender.

Clients consistently rate the work 5 out of 5 for quality and communication — and I bring that same standard to every project, from first concept to final files.
